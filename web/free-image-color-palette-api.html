Free color palette from an image

Drop an image and get its main colors as hex and RGB with the share of the picture each one covers, the average color and a 16 pixel placeholder for lazy loading. The form calls the same free API your code can call: one POST, no API key and no account.

The whole API
curl -s -X POST https://aisenseapi.com/services/v1/image_colors \
  -F "file=@photo.jpg" \
  -F "count=6"

Try it in the browser

This form posts your image to the same endpoint and shows the stored result. The image is sent to aisenseapi.com, and the result is kept for 24 hours at a link that anyone with the link can open.

Drop an image here or click to choose one, or paste it with Ctrl+V. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB.

How the colors are found

  • The image is made small first, at most 128 pixels on its longest side, so a large photo is as quick as a small one.
  • ImageMagick reduces it to the number of colors you ask for, 2 to 16 and 8 when you ask for none, without dithering, and counts the pixels of each. A color's share is its part of the visible pixels. An image with fewer colors gives fewer.
  • Transparent pixels are left out and counted as transparent_share, so a logo on a transparent background gives the colors of the logo.
  • The average is the mean color of the visible pixels of the reduced image.
  • The placeholder is the image at most 16 pixels on its longest side, as a PNG data URI, to show while the real image loads.
  • The image is turned upright first, and a CMYK JPEG is converted to RGB.

The API

POST https://aisenseapi.com/services/v1/image_colors with multipart/form-data.

FieldRequiredMeaning
fileyesThe image: JPEG, PNG or WebP.
countnoHow many colors, 2 to 16. 8 when left out.

The palette is stored as colors.json, and the answer holds the Storage fields and the main points:

FieldMeaning
storage_id, storage_urlWhere the result is. A GET on the URL returns it.
sha256_hash, bytesThe digest and size of the stored result.
expire_timestamp, expire_datetimeWhen the result is removed, 24 hours after it was stored.
content_type, filenameapplication/json and colors.json.
operationimage_colors.
average, dominantThe average color and the color with the largest share, as hex.
countHow many colors the palette holds.

Anyone with the link can read the palette until it expires. Stored results count against the Storage budget of 80 MB per IP address per day.

Limits

  • The upload is a JPEG, PNG or WebP of at most 10 MB and 25 megapixels.
  • One request may take at most 45 seconds.
  • At most two images are worked on at a time, together with the image converter and image compression. A third waits up to ten seconds and is then answered 503 with Retry-After.

Errors

A refused request stores nothing and answers with error. The endpoint's own refusals also carry fix, a sentence saying what to send instead.

StatusWhen
400A field is unknown or out of range, the upload is not one whole file, or ImageMagick cannot read the image.
405The method is not POST.
413The upload, its number of pixels or the result is over a limit.
415The body is not multipart/form-data, or the file is not a JPEG, PNG or WebP.
429More than 5000 requests from one IP address per day, or the day's Storage budget is used up.
503Two images are being worked on already, the work took more than 45 seconds, or the service cannot do it right now.

Questions

Is the color palette API free?

Yes. No API key, no account and no sign up. The limit is 5000 requests per IP address per day, and stored results count against the Storage budget of 80 MB per IP address per day.

How many colors can I get?

2 to 16, and 8 when you do not say. An image with fewer colors gives fewer.

What happens to transparent pixels?

They are left out of the palette and the average, and transparent_share says how much of the image they cover.

Can I use the placeholder in an img tag?

Yes. It is a PNG data URI, so it works as the src of an img element or as a CSS background, with no extra request.

Web utility - Scheduling

Webhook Schedule API

Ask us to call your URL later. POST a target and a payload with a delay or a fire time, and the payload is delivered at that moment - the timer an autonomous process does not have on its own.

  • No API key
  • 5s to 24h
  • One retry
  • SSRF-guarded

Schedule a delivery

POST/webhook_schedule

https://aisenseapi.com/services/v1/webhook_schedule

Schedule a webhook

curl -X POST https://aisenseapi.com/services/v1/webhook_schedule \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"url": "https://example.com/hook", "delay_seconds": 1200, "payload": {"job": 42}}'
{
  "ok": true,
  "schedule_id": "1f1d7b8b-...",
  "status": "scheduled",
  "fire_at_datetime": "2026-08-17T15:21:37+00:00",
  "result_url": "https://aisenseapi.com/services/v1/webhook_schedule/1f1d7b8b-..."
}

Send delay_seconds or a fire_at unix timestamp. The fire time must be between 5 seconds and 24 hours from now.

Check the result

curl https://aisenseapi.com/services/v1/webhook_schedule/1f1d7b8b-...
{ "status": "fired", "attempts": 1, "http_status": 200, "fired_at_datetime": "2026-08-17T15:21:40+00:00" }

status moves from scheduled to fired or failed. Fired means a delivery was attempted and the target answered with some HTTP status, recorded in http_status - a target that returns 500 is still fired. A transport failure is retried once, then failed. Results stay readable for 24 hours after the final attempt.

What it will not call

The target must be a public http or https URL on port 80 or 443. Private, loopback, link-local and reserved addresses are refused - at creation and again at delivery time, with the connection pinned to the address that was checked, so a hostname cannot pass validation and then quietly repoint at an internal service. Redirects are never followed, and credentials in the URL are rejected.

This is what keeps a public scheduler from becoming a tool for reaching inside private networks.

When a delayed callback helps

Agent wait states

Let an autonomous process pause and be woken by a callback instead of holding a connection.

Timeouts and reminders

Schedule a follow-up that fires unless something cancels it first.

Retry after a delay

Re-trigger a step a few minutes after a transient failure.

Deferred notifications

Send a message at a specific later time without running a scheduler yourself.

Privacy and limits

The payload is stored until delivery and for 24 hours after. Do not schedule secrets you would not want held for a day. The horizon is capped at 24 hours because this is a 24-hour service throughout.

The service-wide limit is 5000 requests per IP per 24 hours.

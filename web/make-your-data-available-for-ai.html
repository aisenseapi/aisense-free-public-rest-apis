Make Your Data Available For AI Providers

Many people and companies have valuable text, descriptions or knowledge that AI models never see. This service makes it easy to publish that information.

  • No setup
  • No coding
  • Common document formats

Purpose

Submit text or common document formats such as PDF, Word, Excel and TXT. The service extracts and structures the content, then publishes it at a public address for 180 days. Publication does not guarantee indexing, retrieval or use in model training.

How It Works

1. Submit text or a document

Use one of the document formats supported by the upload form.

2. The system analyzes, extracts and cleans the content

The output includes cleaned text, a summary, keywords, document classification and structured metadata.

3. Structured metadata is produced

The metadata includes structured information and links back to the original source, so AI systems can read and understand it without guessing at context.

4. Publish at a public address

Search engines and AI crawlers may read the published content. Their indexing and use decisions are outside this service's control.

Start Here

Go to data.aisenseapi.com and use the submission form. Review the publication notice before uploading. No coding is needed.

Why This Exists

AI models learn from public information. However, much valuable content never reaches them because it is locked inside closed formats or hidden behind websites that machines cannot read. This service solves that problem by making your text structured, open and accessible to AI systems.

What You Can Share

Only share public and non-confidential content. Anything published here is intended to be read and indexed by third-party AI crawlers, and that cannot be undone once it has been picked up.

After You Upload

The system extracts and cleans the text and publishes structured content and metadata at a public URL. The service retains it for 180 days. Copies made by third-party crawlers may remain outside this service after that period.

What You Do Not Need

No technical knowledge. No special file formats. No hosting or API setup.

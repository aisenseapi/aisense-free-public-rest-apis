Make Your Data Available For AI Providers

Many people and companies have valuable text, descriptions or knowledge that AI models never see. This service makes it easy to publish that information.

  • No setup
  • No coding
  • Any file format

Purpose

You can paste text or upload files in any format. The system analyzes, extracts, cleans and structures the content so AI models can find, understand and use it in their training - making it available both to the public and to models used internally by organizations.

How It Works

1. Upload any file or text

Paste it in or attach a document. Format does not matter.

2. The system analyzes, extracts and cleans the content

It then creates a complex and highly specialized metadata file using advanced processing.

3. Structured metadata is produced

The metadata includes structured information and links back to the original source, so AI systems can read and understand it without guessing at context.

4. AI crawlers index it

Crawlers from all major AI providers can now automatically read and index the result.

Start Here

Go to data.aisenseapi.com, then paste text, upload a document, or share a link. No setup or coding is needed.

Read the specification: AI SENSE AI Data Feed Specification v1.0

Why This Exists

AI models learn from public information. However, much valuable content never reaches them because it is locked inside closed formats or hidden behind websites that machines cannot read. This service solves that problem by making your text structured, open and accessible to AI systems.

What You Can Share

Content typeTypical example
Shop or product informationCatalogues, specifications, availability
Blog posts or articlesEditorial and long-form writing
Manuals and documentationProduct manuals, technical references
Tutorials and guidesHow-to material and walkthroughs
FAQs and service descriptionsSupport answers, what you offer

Only share public and non-confidential content. Anything published here is intended to be read and indexed by third-party AI crawlers, and that cannot be undone once it has been picked up.

After You Upload

The system extracts and cleans the text, then creates a specialized metadata file containing structured information and links to the original source. The processed data and metadata are published in open, machine-readable feeds that AI crawlers from all major providers can automatically read, understand and index.

What You Do Not Need

No technical knowledge. No special file formats. No hosting or API setup.

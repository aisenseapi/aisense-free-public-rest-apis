What you are getting

A set of utility endpoints, provided by AI SENSE AS (Postboks 1202 Vika, 0110 Oslo, Norway, org.nr NO 922 601 151 MVA), free of charge and without registration. Using them means accepting what is on this page. If you do not, do not call them.

They are published under the MIT licence together with client libraries and tests at github.com/aisenseapi/aisense-free-public-rest-apis. That licence covers the code we publish, not the operation of this service.

Limits

5000 requests per IP address per day. Beyond that you receive HTTP 429 until the shared counter resets at midnight Norwegian time (Europe/Oslo). This is not a rolling per-request window. The limit is a technical control. We may change it.

80 MB of stored data per IP address per day on /storage, counted on the same calendar day and reset together with the request counter. Executable files are refused at upload.

Active data has a 24-hour ceiling in /storage, /url_shortener, /webhook_capture, /webhook_action, /agent_wake, /heartbeat, /lease, /inbox and /dns. Agent Wake may use a shorter timeout. A Heartbeat terminal record can remain readable for another 24 hours after its target data has been removed. A Lease has a fixed absolute expiry 24 hours after the first acquisition, and renewals cannot extend it. Webhook Schedule keeps its final result for up to 24 hours after the delivery attempt.

Agent Queue and all associated payloads, deduplication entries, completed jobs and failed jobs expire exactly 24 hours after queue creation. No activity extends that deadline. Each queue accepts up to 100 distinct jobs over its lifetime, 16 KiB of encoded JSON per payload and five claim attempts per job. New queues are limited to 20 per client IP per 24 hours. Queue REST deployment was verified on 9 September 2026.

Jobs can be delivered again after a claim expires or is released. Queue expiry and the attempt limit may leave jobs unfinished. Initial delivery and exactly-once execution are not guaranteed. Make external actions idempotent. The service does not execute jobs, fetch payload URLs or send callbacks.

Queue content also stays yours. Its separate read, write and worker tokens grant access only to their corresponding operations. Workers receive payloads when claiming. Keep these tokens and claim receipts private. A queue ID alone does not authorize access. The Queue service writes payload content to its JSON state files without encrypting it. It normalizes object-key ordering and JSON serialization.

This is not storage you can rely on

The temporary endpoints exist for moving data between steps of a process, not for keeping it. Keep your own copy of anything you cannot afford to lose. Data may become unavailable before the 24 hours elapse - a restart, a fault or maintenance will do it - and we will not be able to recover it for you.

Wallet generation

The wallet endpoints are for development and testing only. They generate a private key on our server and send it to you over the internet. A key produced that way has crossed infrastructure that neither you nor we fully control, and it has existed in memory on a shared public service.

Never place funds in a wallet generated by these endpoints. If you do so anyway, any loss is yours. Balance lookups are informational and may be wrong, stale or unavailable - they depend on third-party blockchain services that we do not operate.

Content you send

Whatever you post to the storage or webhook endpoints stays yours. We claim no rights in it. We store it for the stated period so that we can hand it back to you, and we do nothing else with it. See the Privacy page for what is recorded and for how long.

For Storage, Capture, Approval, Schedule, Agent Wake, Heartbeat and Inbox reads, the relevant bearer ID or link authorizes access. Anyone holding that value can read the associated state, so treat it as a secret. Lease and Queue have their own operation-specific access rules. Queue requires the corresponding read, write or worker token, and a queue ID alone grants no access.

Third parties

Some endpoints contact another service in order to answer you, including blockchain data providers for balance lookups. Short-link creation stores its mapping within this service and does not call a separate redirect provider. Webhook Schedule, Heartbeat and optional Capture or Approval notifications can send requests to public URLs you choose. Agent Inbox receives mail through the configured mail provider. External services have their own terms and availability, and we make no promises on their behalf. The full list is on the Privacy page.
